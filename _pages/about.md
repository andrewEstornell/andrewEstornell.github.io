I work on the alignment and preparedness of LLMs and AI agents, with the goal of making increasingly capable systems safe by design. Over the past three years, my work has focused on LLM post-training and evaluation, for both safety and capability. My earlier research studied the dynamics of agent behavior in AI systems.
